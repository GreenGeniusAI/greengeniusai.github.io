How It Works

Sense. Decide. Water.

Every time

Four steps.

The last one stops a slow-draining pot from being watered twice.

01

Measure

Soil, light, air and water level.

02

Compare

Soil against the plant's range.

03

Water

A dose to the roots, if needed.

04

Re-check

Measure again before anything else.
