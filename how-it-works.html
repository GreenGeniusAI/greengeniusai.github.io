How It Works

From a soil reading to a watered plant.

GreenGenius runs a simple loop on the pot itself: read the sensors, compare with the plant's needs, and water only when the numbers say so.

The system

Sensors, controller, pump.

Every input and output connects to one circuit board in the pot. Decisions are made there, so watering doesn't depend on your phone or your Wi-Fi.

The watering cycle

Four steps, every time.

  1. Measure

    Both soil probes are read, along with light, temperature, humidity and the reservoir level.

  2. Compare

    The moisture reading is placed within this pot's own range, from its dry reading to fully watered, and checked against the plant's band.

  3. Water

    If moisture is below the band and the reservoir has water, the pump delivers a dose to the drip stake at the roots.

  4. Re-check

    The soil is measured again before anything else happens, which prevents a second dose while water is still soaking in.

Plant profiles

Care instructions, turned into ranges.

Plant labels describe watering in words. GreenGenius maps each description to a moisture band, measured as a share of the pot's own range so the same profile works in any soil mix. Choose a care type to see its band.

Light

Light is measured the same way.

The light sensor records brightness through the day. Midday readings are sorted into the tiers used in indoor-plant guidance, so you can tell whether a plant is in the right spot.

TierMidday luxTypical position
Too darkunder 270Hallways, back of a room

Tiers follow UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions indoor light guidance, converted from foot-candles to lux.
