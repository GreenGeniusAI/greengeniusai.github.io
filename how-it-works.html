How It Works

Sense. Decide. Water.

One loop, running on the pot itself. Read the sensors, compare with the plant's needs, and water only when the numbers say so.

The system

Every wire leads to one board.

Decisions are made on the pot, so watering never depends on a phone or a Wi-Fi connection.

The cycle

Four steps, every time.

The last step is the one most systems skip. Measuring again stops a slow-draining pot from getting a second dose it doesn't need.

  1. Step 01

    Measure

    Both soil probes, plus light, temperature, humidity and the reservoir level.

  2. Step 02

    Compare

    Moisture is placed within this pot's own range, dry to fully watered, and checked against the plant's band.

  3. Step 03

    Water

    If moisture is below the band and the reservoir has water, the pump sends a dose to the roots.

  4. Step 04

    Re-check

    The soil is measured again before anything else happens.

Light

Light, sorted into tiers.

The light sensor records brightness through the day. Midday readings are placed in the tiers used by indoor-plant guidance, so a plant in the wrong spot gets noticed.
