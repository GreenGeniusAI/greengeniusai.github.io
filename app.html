What's in it

Six screens, and that's the whole app.

We cut everything that didn't answer a question you'd actually ask.

Live vitals

Moisture, light, temperature and humidity as of the last half-hourly reading — over Wi-Fi from anywhere, or Bluetooth when you're in the room.

Two notifications

Refill the reservoir, and something's wrong. That's the entire notification set by default — no daily engagement pings.

History

Ninety days of readings, charted. The drying curve is the useful one — it shows a struggling root system weeks before the leaves do.

Why it did that

Every watering event records the reading that triggered it and the dose delivered. You can always see the reasoning, not just the result.

Manual dose

Override the autopilot with a measured pour of your choosing, whether you're in the kitchen or another time zone.

Multiple planters

One list, every Terra One in the house, each with its own species profile and history.

Connectivity

Two radios, and neither one is load-bearing.

Bluetooth for setup

Pairing happens locally, standing next to the planter. No cloud round-trip and no account to create before the device will work.

Wi-Fi for remote access

Add your network and you can check in from anywhere. Skip it and the app still works over Bluetooth whenever you're home.

Offline is a supported state

If your router dies, Terra One keeps sensing and watering on its stored profile. It buffers ninety days of readings and syncs them when the network returns.

Data

What we collect, stated plainly.

If you use Bluetooth only, readings never leave your phone. If you enable Wi-Fi, we store sensor readings and watering events against a device ID so the app can chart history and so we can debug faults you report.

We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers, and you can export or delete everything from the app's settings screen. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

The app is free, permanently.

It ships with every Terra One. The planter is the only thing you ever pay for.
