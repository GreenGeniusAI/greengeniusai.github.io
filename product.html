What it does

Three jobs, handled by the pot.

Each one runs on the device. None of them needs a phone to keep a plant alive.

01

Monitors the plant's conditions.

Soil moisture from two probes, plus light, air temperature and humidity, measured throughout the day.

02

Waters from its own reservoir.

When moisture drops below the plant's band, the pump delivers a dose to the roots, then the soil is measured again.

03

Looks after its water supply.

The level sensor reports what's left, asks for a refill in time, and keeps the pump from running dry.

Indoors or out

Built for the balcony too.

v3 was designed with outdoor use in mind. The electronics sit in a sealed bay, and everything that has to breathe or drain does it away from the rain.
