Our Product

GreenGenius

A plant pot with a reservoir, a pump and five sensors built in. Pot the plant, choose its care type, and keep the reservoir filled.

What it does

Three jobs, handled by the pot.

  1. 01

    Monitors conditions

    Soil moisture from two probes, plus light, air temperature and humidity, measured continuously on the device.

  2. 02

    Waters from its reservoir

    When the soil drops below your plant's band, the pump delivers water to a drip stake at the roots, then re-checks the soil.

  3. 03

    Tracks its water supply

    The level sensor reports how much is left in the reservoir and stops the pump from running when it's empty.

Using it

What you do, and what it does.

Set up

Pot your plant, push the soil probes and drip stake into the soil, fill the reservoir and connect USB-C power.

Choose the care type

Pick the plant's watering profile, such as "top inch dry" or "keep evenly moist". The pot uses the matching moisture band.

Refill when asked

Watering is automatic. Your regular task is topping up the reservoir when the level sensor says it's low.

Availability

In development.

GreenGenius is not for sale yet. The circuit board is designed and the enclosure and software are in progress. Pilot units come next.
