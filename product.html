Compatibility

Built around the plants people actually own.

The species library ships with 42 profiles covering the most common houseplants, each defined by a target moisture band and a light range rather than a watering interval. If your plant isn't listed, a custom profile lets you set the band yourself.

Terra One fits nursery pots up to 15 cm in diameter, or plants transferred directly into the soil column.
