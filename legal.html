Privacy policy

What we collect

Only three categories of data:

  • Contact details — the email address you give us when you reserve a unit or join the mailing list. Nothing else is required.
  • Device readings — if, and only if, you connect a Terra One to Wi-Fi: sensor readings, watering events and firmware version, stored against a random device identifier. If you use Bluetooth only, these never leave your phone.
  • Basic site analytics — aggregate page views and referrers, without cookies and without cross-site tracking.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, address or payment details until you actually place an order. There is no camera or microphone in Terra One, so there is no audio or image data of any kind. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build profiles, and we do not require an account to use the product.

What we do with it

Email addresses are used to send launch updates and, for reservation holders, order and shipping notices. Device readings power the history charts in your app and let us reproduce faults you report to support. Analytics tell us which pages people read.

We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with the processors that operate the service on our behalf — our hosting provider, our email provider and our app-store platforms — each bound by contract to use it for nothing else.

How long we keep it

Device readings are retained for 24 months, then deleted. Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Support correspondence is kept for three years for warranty purposes.

Your rights

You can export or delete all of your device data from the app's settings screen, at any time, without contacting us. Every marketing email has a working one-click unsubscribe link. To request a copy of everything we hold, or to have it erased, email privacy@greengenius.co and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, this covers your GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability; if you're in California, it covers your CCPA rights, and we confirm we do not sell personal information.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Firmware updates are cryptographically signed and Terra One will refuse an unsigned image. If we ever suffer a breach affecting your data, we will tell you directly and promptly, not via a footnote in a release note.

Reservation terms

A reservation is not a purchase

Reserving a Terra One costs nothing and commits you to nothing. No payment method is collected at reservation. It places you in the queue and holds founder's pricing of $89 for you against the first 500 units. When units are ready, we email you an invitation to complete an actual purchase, and you decide then.

Cancelling

Reply to any email we send, or email hello@greengenius.co. Your reservation and your email address are removed. There is no charge and no process to work through.

Dates and specifications may change

Terra One is pre-production hardware. The Q4 2026 date is our genuine current estimate, not a guarantee, and tooling or radio certification could move it. Specifications published on this site describe revision 5 and may change before mass production. If the ship date moves or a material specification changes, we will email everyone holding a reservation and explain what happened.

Warranty

Terra One carries a two-year limited hardware warranty from delivery, covering defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from misuse, disassembly beyond the documented repair procedures, or use outside the stated operating conditions. We commit to stocking spare pumps, probes and cartridges for seven years from launch, sold at cost. This warranty is in addition to, and does not limit, your statutory consumer rights.

If we wind down

If GreenGenius ceases operations, we commit to publishing a final firmware release that removes the dependency on our servers, so that Terra One continues to sense and water on-device and the app continues to work over Bluetooth. Watering already runs entirely on-device and never requires our servers to function.
