GreenGenius is a hardware company in Portland, Oregon. We started in 2024 with a windowsill of dead basil and a question that turned out to be harder than it looked: why is watering a plant still guesswork?

Why we're doing this

Boring problems, properly solved.

Self-watering planters have existed for decades. Most of them are a wick and a saucer, and most of the "smart" ones are a moisture probe on a Bluetooth timer. Both fail the same way: they act on a schedule instead of on what the soil is doing.

Closing that loop properly — measure, dose, verify — took us four hardware revisions and eighteen months of units running in strangers' living rooms. It is not a glamorous problem. It is a solvable one, and nobody had bothered to solve it at a price a normal person would pay.

Terra One is the result. It is the only product we make, and it will be for a while.

How we operate

Three commitments we've written down.

01

You own what you bought

No subscription, and no feature moved behind one later. If we ever shut down, the final firmware release will remove the cloud dependency entirely so the hardware keeps working.

02

Repairable for seven years

Pumps, probes and cartridges are user-replaceable, sold at cost, and stocked for seven years from launch. The base opens with a Torx driver, not glue.

03

Published numbers or none

Every figure on this site names its source and sample size. Where we don't have data yet, we say so instead of rounding up to something more impressive.

Roadmap

What's shipped, and what hasn't.

2024 — Complete

First working prototype

Breadboard sensor array and an open-loop pump. It watered on a timer and flooded things. Useful mainly for proving the closed loop was the actual product.

2025 — Complete

Revs 2 to 4, and the home pilot

Custom ESP32 board, isolated analog grounds, and the dose-wait-verify step that ended the flooding. Forty units placed in homes from May 2025.

Feb 2026 — Complete

Seed round and rev 5

$2.4 M closed. Production-intent design frozen, manufacturing partner selected, tooling started.

Q3 2026 — In progress

Certification and first article

FCC and CE testing, tooling trials, and a first-article run to validate the line before mass production.

Q4 2026 — Planned

Founder's edition ships

First 2,000 units to reservation holders, US only. The app ships alongside on iOS and Android.

2027 — Planned

EU shipping and species library v2

European certification and distribution, plus an expanded profile set built from the first year of anonymised field data.
