About Us

We're building a pot that does the checking for you.

Keeping a plant healthy depends on information most people can't see, starting with what's happening in the soil. GreenGenius exists to measure that and act on it.

How we work

Principles behind the product.

  1. 01

    Measure before acting.

    Every watering decision starts from a sensor reading and is checked by another one afterwards.

  2. 02

    Show where the numbers come from.

    Light tiers come from published extension guidance, and moisture bands are derived from documented research on container soils. We cite them rather than present them as our own.

  3. 03

    Decide on the device.

    The pot waters on its own. The app adds information and control, but the plant doesn't depend on a phone or an internet connection.
