The problem

Plants don't follow a schedule.

Watering is the hardest part of plant care to get right. Too much and the roots sit wet; too little and the plant dries out. The difficulty is knowing what the soil needs on a given day.

  1. 01

    Soil dries at a different rate every week.

    Light, season, heating, pot size and soil mix all change how fast water leaves the pot. A fixed watering day can't account for any of them.

  2. 02

    Care advice is hard to act on.

    "Water when the top inch is dry" is good advice, but checking it by hand every day is easy to skip and hard to judge.

  3. 03

    Problems show up late.

    Wilting and yellowing leaves appear days after the soil was too wet or too dry. By then, the cause is already in the past.

Introducing GreenGenius

Care based on measurements, not memory.

GreenGenius is a plant pot with sensors, a water reservoir and a small pump built in. It checks conditions throughout the day and handles watering on its own, so your part is choosing the plant and refilling the reservoir.

It measures the soil.

Two moisture probes sit in the root zone, alongside sensors for light, temperature and humidity.

It waters itself.

When moisture drops below your plant's range, the pump delivers water from the reservoir directly to the roots.

It tells you what it needs.

A water-level sensor tracks the reservoir, so you know when to refill before it runs dry.

How it works

Sense, decide, water.

Sensors feed a controller on the pot's own circuit board. It compares readings with your plant's moisture range and runs the pump only when they fall short.

  1. Measure

    Soil moisture, light, temperature, humidity and reservoir level are read on the device.

  2. Compare

    Moisture is checked against the range for your plant, scaled to this pot's own wet and dry readings.

  3. Water

    If the soil is below range, the pump moves water from the reservoir to a drip stake in the soil.

  4. Re-check

    The soil is measured again before any further watering, so slow-draining soil isn't watered twice.
